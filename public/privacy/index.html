927Dev Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Bot: 927Dev (Discord application ID 1453088174409515243)

Operator: Keyno Gotti, for the Baddie Heights / 927 FiveM development and community Discord servers

What 927Dev is

927Dev is a management bot for our FiveM development and community Discord servers. It helps with moderation, tickets, role syncing, logging and routing staff and bug reports, and general server management.

What data it accesses

In servers where 927Dev is added, it can access:

  • Discord user IDs, usernames and display names
  • Server roles and role changes
  • Member join and leave events
  • Message content in channels it can see
  • Presence / online status

Why

This data is used only to run the bot's features: moderation, support tickets, role syncing, logging and routing staff and bug reports, and server management.

Storage and retention

  • We store only what is needed for those features.
  • Logs are kept for a limited time and then deleted.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.

Deleting your data

To ask about or request deletion of your data, contact staff in the Baddie Heights Discord: https://discord.gg/baddieheightsrp

Children

927Dev is meant for Discord users who meet Discord's Terms of Service age requirement (13+, or older where local law requires).

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are posted here with a new effective date. Continuing to use servers where 927Dev runs means you accept the updated policy.
